A LITTLE CLARITY

Before you
get started.

The product, the next step, and how it fits your business.

What is Techabanca Catalogue?+

A separate web application for organizing your products and services, preparing a catalogue website, and managing customer enquiries. It brings business information, item content and website controls into one workspace.

Can I catalogue services as well as products?+

Yes. Choose products, services, or both during business setup. Organize the items into categories and add fields that fit your business.

Do I need to design a website from scratch?+

No. Catalogue includes a managed professional theme and guided setup. You prepare business information, imagery and content, then use the preview and publication workflow subject to its availability.

What is available in early access?+

Account creation and workspace setup are available, and the published Example Industries pilot can be explored on this page. Wider public website activation is being finalized. Creating an account does not publish a website, start a trial, or activate a paid subscription.

How much does it cost?+

Creating your workspace requires no payment method. Eligible owners and admins can explicitly start the optional 14-day trial from Subscription, with no card or automatic paid renewal. Paid plans and their prices are not available yet; check the application’s Pricing area for current availability.

Can I work from my phone?+

Yes. Catalogue adapts to desktop and mobile browsers. Use the same Catalogue account and workspace; there is no separate app to install.

How does it fit alongside Techabanca Billing?+

Billing focuses on sales documents, purchases, expenses and business reports. Catalogue focuses on presenting your offer and managing its content. Each application has its own workspace; automatic synchronization of records between the two is not currently offered.
